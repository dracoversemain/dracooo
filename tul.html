How it works

Four steps between "I have no idea where to start" and a brand guide you'd actually hand to a client.

01

Take the quiz

Twenty questions on your values, audience, and industry. Ten minutes, no design vocabulary required.

02

Review your system

See three to five palette directions, paired typography, and a logo style brief built from your answers.

03

Preview it live

Check the system on a business card, a website header, and a social post before you commit to anything.

04

Export everything

CSS variables, Adobe swatches, a Figma file, and a PDF guide — pick the formats your workflow actually uses.
